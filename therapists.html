Mental Health Resources

Find support, therapy, and mental health services.

You are not alone. Help is here

Brightside

Brightside provides online therapy and psychiatry for anxiety, depression, and other mental health conditions. Brightside offers personalized treatment plans and resources designed to help better understand and manage their mental health

Grow Therapy

Grow Therapy helps you find licensed therapists and psychiatrists who accept many insurance plans. The platform makes it easier for people to find a provider who fits their individual needs.

Open Path

Connects users with licensed mental health professionals for online therapy and counseling. It also offers self-help tools and resources to support emotional well-being. Open path makes therapy more accessible to people who have a difficult time paying for traditional mental health services.

Talk Space

Talkspace offers online therapy and psychiatry through messaging, video, and audio sessions with licensed professionals.

Rula

Rula matches licensed therapists and psychiatry providers for individuals, couples, and family therapy, as well as medication management.

Welltrack-Connect

Connects users with licensed mental health professionals for online therapy and counseling. Offers self-help tools and resources to support emotional well-being.

Mentalhealthmatch

Matches you with a licensed therapist based on your needs, preferences, insurance, and location to help you find the right mental health provider.

Teladoc Health

Provides virtual therapy and psychiatry appointments with licensed mental health professionals. Users can receive counseling and, when appropriate, medication management online.

Joyous

Offers guided low-dose therapy and mental health support throught licensed medical professionals in a safe, supportive environment

7cups

Provides free emotional support through trained volunteer listeners and offers online therapy with licensed mental health professionals.
