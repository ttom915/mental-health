Mission and Values

Connect Within is a site created to guide you towards free mental health resources and support spaces, including places that provide therapy. Please take advantage of the free resources available to you! Our organization believes that mental health is important for everyone, regardless of personal or political beliefs. We want you to feel healthy, fresh, and confident in yourself. It is okay and perfectly normal to ask for and seek help!
